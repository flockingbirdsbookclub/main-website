Before you ask

Why a deposit and not the full price?

A deposit is what tells the printer this order is real, and it keeps the risk small for you. You pay the balance when you collect and can see the thing in your hands.

What if my size is wrong?

Come and try before you pay the balance. We will hold sizes back where we can, but on a print-to-order run there is no warehouse to swap from, so choose carefully.

Can I order if I am not a member?

Yes. The Social on 3 October is open to everyone, and so is the merch.

What happens if not enough people order?

If an item does not hit its minimum run we will tell you and refund the deposit in full. No item gets quietly dropped.

I cannot make it on 3 October.

Say so when you send your order and we will sort out collection or delivery with you directly.
